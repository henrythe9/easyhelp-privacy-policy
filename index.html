Overview

All data you create is stored on your device only.

EasyHelp is a local-first location manager. It saves, organises, and shares GPS locations via SMS, email, or photo. We do not operate any server, do not create user accounts, and do not transmit your personal data to any server we control.

What Data We Collect

Data We DO NOT Collect

Data typeStatus
Your name, email, or account credentials❌ Not collected
Your location history or movement patterns❌ Not collected
Your contacts list❌ Not stored — contact picker returns a phone number for one-time use
Device identifiers (beyond AdMob — see below)❌ Not collected by us
Analytics or crash reports❌ Not collected
Any data sent to our servers❌ We have no servers

Data Stored Locally on Your Device (never leaves your phone)

  • Saved locations — names, GPS coordinates, and addresses you explicitly save
  • Your contact info — your own phone number and email address (entered in Settings, used to populate Share Location messages)
  • Custom message templates — the SMS/email message templates you create in Settings
  • App preferences — theme (light/dark), sort order

All of this data lives in encrypted local storage on your device. It is never uploaded anywhere. You can delete it at any time from Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions Used and Why

All runtime permissions show an explanation dialog before the system request. If you deny a permission, the related feature is gracefully disabled; the rest of the app continues to work.

Third-Party Services

Google AdMob

EasyHelp displays banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use:

  • Your device's Advertising ID (AD_ID)
  • General device information (model, OS version)
  • Approximate location (country/region level, derived from IP address)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google LLC under their own privacy policy. You can review it at:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can opt out of personalised ads at any time on Android via:
Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or "Opt out of Ads Personalisation" on older Android)

We do not receive any personally identifiable information from AdMob.

Geocoding (reverse geocoding service)

When you refresh your location, the app converts GPS coordinates to a human-readable address using a geocoding service. This request contains only coordinates (no name, account, or identifier). It is a one-way lookup; results are displayed on screen and are not stored on any server by us.

Demo Mode

The Demo Mode (accessible from Settings) loads 10 San Francisco sample locations entirely in memory. No demo data is ever written to your device's local storage. When you exit Demo Mode, all demo data is discarded immediately.

Data Security

  • All app data is stored in encrypted local storage on your device.
  • No data is transmitted to servers we operate.
  • Backup files created by the app (via Settings → Backup) are saved to your device's local storage. You control where they are saved and who has access.
  • We recommend protecting your device with a screen lock as a general security measure.

Children's Privacy

EasyHelp does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app does not require account registration and does not transmit personal data. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through this app, please contact us via the in-app feedback feature and we will take appropriate action.
