EasyHelp — Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 26, 2026  |  App: EasyHelp Location Manager

Overview

EasyHelp is a local-first location manager. It saves, organises, and shares GPS locations via SMS, email, or photo. All data you create is stored on your device only. We do not operate any server, do not create user accounts, and do not transmit your personal data to any server we control.

What Data We Collect

Data We Do Not Collect

Data typeStatus
Your name, email address, or account credentialsNot collected
Your location history or movement patternsNot collected
Your contacts listNot stored — contact picker returns one phone number for one-time use
Device identifiers (beyond AdMob — see below)Not collected by us
Analytics or crash reportsNot collected
Any data sent to our serversWe have no servers

Data Stored Locally on Your Device (never leaves your phone)

  • Saved locations — names, GPS coordinates, and addresses you explicitly save
  • Your contact info — your own phone number and email (entered in Settings, used only to populate Share Location messages)
  • Custom message templates — the SMS/email templates you create in Settings
  • App preferences — theme (light/dark), sort order

All of this data lives in a local encrypted database (Hive) on your device. It is never uploaded anywhere. You can delete it at any time from Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions Used and Why

PermissionWhy it is needed
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION Obtain your current GPS coordinates when you tap "Refresh Location"
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION Fallback location when GPS signal is weak
READ_CONTACTS Contact picker — lets you choose a recipient phone number. The number is used once for sharing; your contacts list is not stored by the app
CAMERA Lets you attach a photo to an email when sharing your location
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Backup and restore saved locations on Android 9 and below only. Not used on Android 10+
INTERNET Required for address lookup (geocoding) and for Google AdMob to display ads
AD_ID Required by Google AdMob to serve personalised advertisements

All runtime permissions display an explanation before the system prompts you. If you deny a permission, the related feature is gracefully disabled; the rest of the app continues to work.

Third-Party Services

Google AdMob

EasyHelp displays banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use:

  • Your device's Advertising ID
  • General device information (model, OS version)
  • Approximate location at country/region level (derived from IP address)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google LLC under their own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

To opt out of personalised ads on Android, go to: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or "Opt out of Ads Personalisation" on older Android versions).

We do not receive any personally identifiable information from AdMob.

Geocoding (Address Lookup)

When you refresh your location, the app converts GPS coordinates to a human-readable address. This request contains only coordinates — no name, account, or identifier. Results are displayed on screen only and are not stored on any server by us.

Demo Mode

The Demo Mode (accessible from Settings) loads 10 sample locations entirely in memory. No demo data is ever written to your device's local database. When you exit Demo Mode, all demo data is discarded immediately.

Data Security

  • All app data is stored in a local database (Hive) on your device.
  • No data is transmitted to servers we operate.
  • Backup files are saved to your device's local storage. You control where they go and who has access.
  • We recommend protecting your device with a screen lock as a general security measure.

Children's Privacy

EasyHelp does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app does not require account registration and does not transmit personal data. If you are a parent or guardian who believes your child has provided personal information through this app, please contact us via the in-app feedback feature and we will take appropriate action.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
